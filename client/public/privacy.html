Article 02

Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to all personal data we collect and process in connection with:

  • Venue owners who register and operate venues on TabPro;

This Policy does not cover the data practices of third-party services we integrate with (such as payment processors or cloud service providers), which are governed by their own privacy policies.

Article 04

Legal Basis for Processing

In accordance with Article 9 of Law No. 058/2021, we rely on the following legal grounds to process personal data:

  • Contractual necessity: Processing required to perform the TabPro service you have registered for or interact with, including order management, billing, and account administration.
  • Consent: Where you have freely and explicitly provided consent, such as when a customer voluntarily enters their phone number to open a tab, or when you opt in to push notifications.
  • Legitimate interests: For analytics, platform security, fraud prevention, and service improvement, where those interests are not overridden by your fundamental rights.
  • Legal obligation: Where we are required to retain or disclose data to comply with applicable Rwandan law, including tax, financial records, and law enforcement requirements.
Article 05

How We Use Your Personal Data

We use the personal data we collect for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage venue owner, staff, and customer accounts;
  • To process orders, payments, and tab transactions;
  • To deliver shift schedules, attendance records, and performance reports to venue owners;
  • To send transactional notifications, including order status updates and clock-in confirmations, via push notification, SMS, or WhatsApp;
  • To process billing and manage subscriptions;
  • To monitor and improve platform security and performance;
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations;
  • To respond to support inquiries and resolve disputes.

We will not use your data for any purpose incompatible with the purposes stated above without first obtaining your consent or having another lawful basis to do so.

Article 06

Sharing Your Personal Data

We do not sell personal data. We may share your data only in the following circumstances:

6.1 — Service Providers

We engage third-party processors who act on our behalf, under binding data processing agreements, to provide infrastructure and services including:

  • Cloud hosting: Render (server hosting and static site deployment);
  • Database: Neon (PostgreSQL cloud database);
  • Media storage: Cloudinary (image uploads);
  • Payment processing: MTN Mobile Money (MoMo) for subscription billing;
  • Messaging: Africa's Talking or equivalent provider for SMS/WhatsApp staff invitations.

6.2 — Venue Owners

Customer order data, tab history, and ratings are visible to the venue owner and their authorised staff within the TabPro platform. Customers acknowledge this when they open a tab at a participating venue.

6.3 — Legal Requirements

We may disclose personal data where required to do so by Rwandan law, court order, or competent regulatory authority, including the Rwanda Data Protection Authority (RPDA) and the Rwanda Investigation Bureau (RIB).

6.4 — Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or part of VNT250 Ltd's assets, personal data may be transferred to the successor entity, subject to the same protections described in this Policy.

Article 08

Your Rights as a Data Subject

Under Law No. 058/2021, you have the following rights in respect of your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at iga.enterprise.ltd@gmail.com.

  • Right of access: You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • Right to rectification: You may request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • Right to erasure: You may request deletion of your personal data where it is no longer necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, subject to legal retention obligations;
  • Right to restrict processing: You may request that we limit how we use your data in certain circumstances;
  • Right to data portability: You may request your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format;
  • Right to object: You may object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing purposes;
  • Right to withdraw consent: Where processing is based on your consent, you may withdraw it at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

We will respond to all valid requests within 30 days of receipt. If you believe your rights have been violated, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Rwanda Data Protection Authority (RPDA).

Article 09

Security Measures

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss, alteration, or disclosure, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit using TLS/HTTPS;
  • Bcrypt hashing of all authentication credentials (staff PINs and venue PINs);
  • JWT-based session tokens with defined expiry windows;
  • Role-based access controls ensuring staff can only access data relevant to their venue and role;
  • Physical clock-in verification requiring physical presence at the venue;
  • Regular security reviews of our codebase and dependencies.

No system can guarantee absolute security. In the event of a personal data breach that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the RPDA and affected data subjects in accordance with applicable law.

Article 10

Cookies and Local Storage

The TabPro platform uses browser local storage and session storage to maintain your authenticated session (for venue owners and waitstaff). These are strictly necessary for the platform to function and do not require separate consent.

We do not use third-party tracking cookies or cross-site advertising technologies. Any analytics we collect are anonymised and aggregated at the venue level.

Article 11

Children's Data

The TabPro platform is not directed at children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from individuals under 16. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, please contact us immediately at iga.enterprise.ltd@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.
