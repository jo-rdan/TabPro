Article 03

Description of the Service

TabPro is a software-as-a-service (SaaS) platform that allows restaurant and hospitality venue owners to:

  • Configure digital menus accessible via QR code;
  • Allow customers to open tabs, browse menus, and place orders without a dedicated mobile application;
  • Manage orders and service in real time through a staff-facing interface;
  • Track staff shifts, attendance, and clock-in via physical QR sticker and PIN verification;
  • Monitor venue performance through dashboards and reports;
  • Receive push notifications for order and service updates.

We reserve the right to modify, enhance, or discontinue any feature of the Service at any time with reasonable notice to Venue Owners.
